name: Chatbot contact and attribute capture
description: User-described product behavior that the chatbot section must demonstrate.
---

When a customer messages a client's WhatsApp account, AtWassup automatically stores the customer's number. It also stores attributes such as name, number, pincode, address, or any other attribute asked by the chatbot or shared during the conversation. The saved data can be used later for marketing.

**Why:** The user described these as actual features of their system and rejected prior booking-focused visuals because they did not communicate these capabilities.

The user explicitly approved the synchronized conversation-to-customer-data capture concept. Keep that behavior intact when refining the presentation.

**How to apply:** Show automatic number capture on the first incoming message and other fields populating only after their corresponding customer messages. Include a custom attribute, not just fixed contact fields. Keep the presentation creative, professional and concise, rather than a complex theoretical feature explanation.
